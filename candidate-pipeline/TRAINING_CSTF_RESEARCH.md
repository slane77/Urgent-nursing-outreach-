## 2. Framework specifics & differences

**CSTF (baseline)** — national standard defining minimum learning outcomes, target
audience and refresher frequency for the 11 subjects, to prevent duplication between
NHS orgs. England Subject Guide **v1.1 (June 2021)**, reviewed annually — cite the
version/date. "CSTF-aligned" (what e-learning providers claim) ≠ Skills for Health
*accreditation*; whether Day Webster's own modules are accredited is the **agency's
own business fact** to state, not inferred.

**HTE (HealthTrust Europe — Total Workforce Solutions III)** — requires all supplied
workers to hold **CSTF-aligned** training + NHS Employment Check Standards; regular
audits. **Distinctive HTE nuance:** a 2024/25 TWS III update mandates that specific
practical modules be completed **FACE-TO-FACE (not e-learning only)** — namely **Fire
Safety, Conflict Resolution, Moving & Handling, and Resuscitation (BLS)**, typically
**annually**. Confirm the exact current HTE module list/levels/frequencies from HTE
TWS III compliance documentation.

**WFA (NHS Workforce Alliance)** — **RM6277 is the *Non-Clinical* staffing framework;
the *clinical/healthcare* route is RM6281 "Clinical & Healthcare Staffing" (renewal of
RM6161)** — if Day Webster supplies clinical staff, RM6281/RM6161 is the relevant lot.
Requires the **11 CSTF modules** + NHS Employers Pre-/Post-Employment Check Standards
(ID, RTW, registration/qualifications, references, DBS, occupational health); regular
audits. The surfaced docs did not specify an HTE-style face-to-face mandate — confirm
[?].

**Bottom line:** all three point to the **same 11 CSTF subjects**; the material
divergence is **delivery/frequency of practical elements** — HTE explicitly mandates
annual face-to-face for Fire/Conflict/Moving & Handling/BLS.
